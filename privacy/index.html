Privacy Policy

No Frills English/Spanish Translator explains here what information we collect, how we use it, and how to contact us about privacy requests.

What we collect

  • We collect the text, audio, and account email needed to provide translation, premium restore, and support services.
  • Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Translation history and premium status may be stored to improve app continuity and restore purchases.
  • You can request support, privacy, or deletion help by emailing Hvygoose@gmail.com.

How we use your information

We use submitted text and audio to generate translations, transcriptions, and speech playback. We may also store translation history and premium status to support app functionality and purchase restoration.

Payments and premium

Premium purchases are processed through Stripe. Stripe may collect and process payment details directly under its own privacy terms. We store limited purchase-related data such as email, session identifiers, and premium activation status.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email Hvygoose@gmail.com from the email address associated with your use of the app. We will delete your translation history, premium records, and any associated personal data within 30 days and confirm by reply. There is no charge for deletion requests.

Data retention and deletion

We retain operational data only as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain history features, investigate support issues, and handle refund or fraud review needs. To request deletion or privacy assistance, email Hvygoose@gmail.com.

Jurisdiction and contact

This service is operated from Nevada, United States. Privacy questions, access requests, and deletion requests should be sent to Hvygoose@gmail.com.
